## About

Prajna Films is an independent film production house based in Mumbai,
devoted to cinematic storytelling rooted in emotion, memory, and
transformation. The name Prajna — derived from Sanskrit, meaning
*wisdom* or *deep understanding* — reflects our belief that meaningful
cinema begins with a profound understanding of the human condition.

We make stories from the margins — for the world.

---

## Founder & Director

**Abhilash Sharma** — Director and Founder, Prajna Films

Abhilash Sharma is a Mumbai-based filmmaker with over 15 years in
Indian cinema. He debuted as a feature director in 2012 with
*Achal Rahe Suhaag*, a modern retelling of the Savitri-Satyavan myth.
His debut film as founder of Prajna Films — *Swaha* (2024) — won two
Golden Goblet Awards at the Shanghai International Film Festival.

## Films

### Swaha (स्वाहा) — 2024
**In The Name of Fire**

- Language: Magahi (ancient Bihar language, 2,500 years old)
- Format: Black & White · 99 Minutes
- World Premiere: Shanghai International Film Festival · June 2024
- India Premiere: International Film Festival of Kerala · December 2024

**Awards:**
- 🏆 Golden Goblet Award — Best Director · Shanghai IFF 2024
- 🏆 Golden Goblet Award — Best Actor (Satya Ranjan) · Shanghai IFF 2024
- Asian New Talent Competition · Shanghai IFF 2024

Starring: Sonalli Sharmisstha & Satya Ranjan
